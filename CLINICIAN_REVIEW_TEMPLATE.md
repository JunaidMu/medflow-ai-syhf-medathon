# MedFlow AI — Clinician Review Template

**For qualified medical reviewers only.** This template is separate from the automated
software test results in `test-results.json` / `TEST_REPORT.md`. The automated results
describe *software behavior under fictional test conditions* and establish nothing about
clinical accuracy, safety, or suitability for use with real patients.

---

## Instructions for the reviewer

1. Read `TEST_REPORT.md` and, for full observed output, `test-results.json` (or open
   `testing.html` and press **Run Tests**).
2. Review each scenario ID below. You may review all 30 or a subset.
3. Fill one row per reviewed scenario in the **Review log** table, or the full form in
   **Detailed review form** for cases needing deeper comments.
4. Save the completed file (for example as `CLINICIAN_REVIEW.md`) alongside the automated
   results. Automated results must not be edited to reflect clinical opinions — the review
   stays a separate, clearly attributed document.
5. By signing the **Reviewer attestation** you confirm your identity and professional
   role. There is no pre-filled reviewer name, signature, or endorsement in this template,
   and none may be fabricated.

> ⚠️ All 30 scenarios currently have the status: **Clinical review status: Not reviewed.**
> This template contains no reviewer name, signature, or approval, and none should be added
> except by a qualified reviewer recording a genuine review.
