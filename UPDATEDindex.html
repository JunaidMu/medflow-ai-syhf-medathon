    How to Use MedFlow AI (Step-by-Step Guide)

    New to the system? Launch the interactive 4-step live tour or explore the dedicated guides for self-check-in patients and emergency department clinicians below.

    FOR PATIENTS · TABLET KIOSK

    👤 Patient Self-Check-In Guide

    Designed to take ~40 seconds at the hospital entrance before seeing the triage nurse.

    01

    Speak Naturally in Arabic or English

    Press 'Record microphone' and describe your symptoms, duration, medications, and allergies. In the Arabic tab (العربية), even if you speak English, OpenAI automatically translates your transcript into Arabic (and vice versa in the EN tab)!

    02

    Use Touch Helpers (Quick Pills & 0–10 Pain Scale)

    Don't want to type in a noisy ER? Tap the quick body-symptom buttons (Chest Pain, Shortness of Breath, Fever) and tap your pain score from 0 (Green) to 10 (Red).

    03

    Tap AI Follow-Up Clarifiers & Confirm Accuracy

    The AI automatically fills your Allergies & Medications boxes and suggests 2–3 purple clarifier chips (e.g., '+ Started < 1 hour ago?'). Tap any chip to add it, then check the confirmation box.

    04

    Get Your QR Zone Ticket & Arabic/English Voice Readout

    Submit the form to receive your boarding pass (Zone A Resuscitation, Zone B Priority, or Zone C Fast-Track) with an OpenAI neural voice readout in both Arabic and English!

    FOR CLINICIANS · TRIAGE & EMR WORKSPACE

    🩺 Clinician & Triage Nurse Guide

    How to review incoming patient intakes, record objective vitals, and export FHIR R4 bundles.

    01

    Live Priority Queue & Hospital Zone Load

    Patients with Immediate red flags automatically jump to the top of the queue. Monitor live capacity across Zone A, B, and C, plus total nurse triage minutes saved.

    02

    3-Color Clinical Provenance & Bilingual SOAP

    Every note section is color-coded so staff never confuse AI output with confirmed facts: Cyan = Patient-reported, Amber = System/AI suggestion, Green = Clinician-confirmed.

    03

    Record Objective Vitals & Override Safety Guardrail

    Enter clinician-measured vitals (HR, BP, SpO2) to populate the Objective section. If you change the AI's suggested urgency, the system enforces a mandatory clinical reason.

    04

    Export NPHIES / HL7 FHIR R4 Bundle & Audit Log

    Click 'Preview FHIR JSON' on any patient chart to generate a standards-compliant HL7 FHIR R4 Document Bundle with LOINC codes, or download the full timestamped audit trail.
